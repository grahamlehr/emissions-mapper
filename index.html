    How this is calculated

    DESNZ 2026
    1. Distance. The great-circle distance between the two airports, plus 8% for indirect routes and delays.
    2. Flight type. Under 500 km uses DESNZ's domestic factor (our cut-off, based on DESNZ's 434 km average UK domestic flight). Under 3,700 km is short-haul; anything longer is long-haul. DESNZ classes UK flights by region, but distance is used here so any pair of airports works.
    3. Cabin. Economy, or business when the estimated flight time is over your policy threshold. Flight time is estimated as distance ÷ 850 km/h + 0.55 h.
    4. Factors. Grams of CO2 per passenger-km:
      Flight typeEconomyBusiness
    5. Non-CO2.
    6. Trips.
    7. Jetlag. Hours of time-zone difference using standard time. Daylight saving is ignored.
    8. Limits. Every journey is treated as one direct flight from the airport assigned to that group. Only CO2 is counted (methane and nitrous oxide are left out), so results are slightly lower than DESNZ's own CO2e figures. This is for comparing options, not for carbon reporting.

    Source: UK Government GHG conversion factors 2026 (DESNZ), published 11 June 2026. Methodology paper tables 36–37, paragraphs 8.37 and 8.42.
